For YouTube creators

Know your real organic watch time

YouTube Studio counts every hour watched on your channel, including the hours you spend checking your own videos, streams and Shorts. YT Activity Tracker shows exactly how much of that came from your phone, so you can see how much is genuine audience growth.

  • See how many hours you watched your own channel, by day, video and playlist
  • Subtract them from YouTube Studio's numbers to get your organic watch time
  • Track honest progress toward the 4,000 public watch hours the YouTube Partner Program asks for
  • Spot which of your videos and streams you keep re-watching instead of making the next one
YouTube filters self-views and artificial watch time, and may reject channels that inflate it. Use the tracker to measure honestly. Great content is what really gets you monetized faster.

Privacy

Your viewing history stays on your phone

  • No sign-in, no ads, no analytics SDKs.
  • Watch history is stored only in the app's private database. Delete it all any time from Settings.
  • Media Playback Access reads only YouTube's "now playing" title, channel and play/pause state. Other notifications are never read.

FAQ

Questions

Does it show my channel's views from YouTube Studio?

No. YT Activity Tracker measures what's watched in the YouTube app on your phone, including your own channel. Compare it with YouTube Studio to see how much of your channel's watch time came from you and how much is organic.

Will watching my own videos help me get monetized?

No. YouTube filters self-views and artificial watch time, and can reject Partner Program applications from channels that inflate it. The tracker helps you see your real organic progress so you can focus on content that grows it.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

The app needs Usage Access and Accessibility permissions that the Play Store restricts heavily. Installing the APK directly is safe. The setup guide shows how, including what to do if Play Protect shows a warning.

Play Protect says the app is unsafe or blocked. Is it?

Play Protect warns about every app installed from outside the Play Store that asks for Accessibility access. Tap More details → Install anyway. If you don't see that option, pause Play Protect scanning while you install and turn it back on afterwards. See the steps.

Which YouTube apps are supported?

The official YouTube app (com.google.android.youtube), including live streams and Shorts. YouTube in a browser, YouTube Music and modded YouTube apps are not tracked.

Why are prices different in my country?

Full Access is priced in Indian rupees (₹) in India and in US dollars ($) everywhere else. Your billing country at checkout decides which price applies, and every plan unlocks the same features.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app, open Settings → Full Access → Manage Subscription, or use the Manage your subscription form to get a link by email. You keep Full Access until the end of the paid period. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

I got a new phone. Do I pay again?

No. Install the app, open Unlock Full Access, enter the same email and tap Activate. One purchase works on up to 3 phones.

Does it drain battery?

No. It uses Android's own screen-time data and YouTube's media info instead of constantly polling. On some phones, allow background activity so it keeps working. How.
