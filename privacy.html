Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

YT Activity Tracker is built so your viewing history stays on your phone. This policy explains exactly what the app and this website handle.

On your phone only

  • Watch history: sessions, video titles, channel and playlist names and durations are stored in the app's private database on your device. We never receive them. You can delete everything in Settings → Delete All Stored Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Usage Access is used to know when the YouTube app is open.
  • Media Playback Access (Android's notification-listener permission) reads only YouTube's "now playing" title, channel and play/pause state. The contents of your other notifications are never read or stored.
  • Accessibility (optional) reads visible text inside the YouTube app only, to detect playlists, Shorts and ads. It never reads other apps, passwords or messages.

What leaves your phone

  • Channel logos: channel names are sent to the YouTube Data API (Google) to fetch channel pictures. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Full Access license: when you activate or re-check Full Access, the app sends your email and an anonymous device ID (Android's per-app ID) to our license server on Cloudflare. We store your email, your plan and its status, the payment or subscription reference from Dodo Payments, and up to 3 device IDs.
  • Payments: handled entirely by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. Dodo collects your payment details, billing address and email under its own privacy policy and shares with us only what's needed to unlock your plan (email, plan, status and transaction references).

This website

No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The pricing page remembers your email in your browser's local storage, only to pre-fill the form. Our server uses your IP address's country only to show local prices, and doesn't store it.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent personal data, show ads, or use your data for profiling.

Retention and your rights

License records are kept while you have Full Access and for as long as needed for accounting, tax and dispute purposes afterwards. You can ask to see, correct or delete your license data at primestategaming@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

Children

The Service isn't directed at children under 13, and Full Access may only be bought by adults or with a parent's permission.

Changes

We'll post updates here and change the date above.
