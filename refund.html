Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Cancelling a subscription

  • Cancel any time in the app: Settings → Full Access → Manage Subscription, or with the Manage your subscription form, which emails you a secure link.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep Full Access until the end of the billing period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again.
  • Uninstalling the app or removing it from a device does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

  • First purchase: if Full Access isn't working for you, ask for a full refund within 7 days of your first payment for any plan, including Lifetime.
  • Renewals: renewal payments aren't refunded, except when you were charged after cancelling, charged twice, or couldn't use Full Access because of a problem on our side. Write to us within 14 days of the charge.
  • Payment problems: duplicate or incorrect charges are always refunded.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method through Dodo Payments, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.
  • Once a payment is refunded, the Full Access it paid for ends.

How to ask

Email primestategaming@gmail.com from the address you paid with, including your payment receipt or order ID. Refunds are processed by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, which may also handle requests made to it directly.
